## Open / tight spots

- **26:4:** נָכוֹן tagged niphal participle “preparado/cierto,” not place-name Nekon (first-pass error).
- **26:11 / 26:16:** Packet `אשר` lacks מראשותיו; left open (“la lanza que…”, “las aguas que—”).
- **26:22:** Packet has הנה המלך (not החנית המלך); calqued “He aquí el rey.”
- **27:8:** Packet has geshurí + amaleqí only (no gizrí in this OSHB token list).
- **27:10:** אַל־פשטתם tagged H408; sense treated as אָן / “¿Adónde se esparcieron?” (common reading).
- **28:3,7–9:** אוב / ידעני → *ov* / *yidoní* (cult terms; AHRC “WINESKIN” non-binding).
- **29:5:** Song line ends at ודוד in packet (no ברבבתיו); left truncated.
- **30:6:** Packet has איש על ועל בנתיו (בניו absent); calqued “hombre sobre y sobre sus hijas.”
- **28:10 / 30:15:** Oath `אם` after `חי־יהוה` / `השבעה … באלהים` rendered as negative pledge (no iniquity / no kill-or-deliver).
- **29:1:** בעין → *fuente* (spring at Yizreel), not “ojo” dump.
- **30:27–31:** Place list Hebraized from packet compounds (Bet El, Ramot Néguev, Bor Ashán, Atac, etc.).
