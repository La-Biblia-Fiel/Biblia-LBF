## 2:8 — why not "vosotros"

The candidate read "vosotros". That is not a Peninsular spelling of "nosotros";
they are different persons translating different Greek words. TR has ὑμῶν
(second person), the critical text has ἡμῶν (first person). Taking the TR
reading in Latin American Spanish gives **"ustedes"**, which is also the form
used throughout the rest of the project. "nosotros" would have silently reverted
to the critical-text reading.
